# The vocabulary supplement — REV 1.2

A working vocabulary for daily speech, coined as new Old Pelagic roots and
**derived by the sound-change engines** (`odylang/vocabulary.py`) — every
form below, in every column, is regular output; nothing is hand-written.
Post-codex supplement: separate from the canon lexicons of docs/01 and
docs/06. Regenerate this file with `python3 -m odylang.cli vocab` or the
script in the module docstring.

## notes

- **shir** — the word for a sibling is the word for the one who shares your dives
- **mār** — *mahin 'hand' with the agent grade: the hand that steers
- **jish** — the *isi-class shape: SC-1 twice then apocope — jish
- **shen** — homophone of shen 'six' by regular change — counting is breathing, the divers say
- **sniv** — final glide hardens: sniv, like nav
- **chir** — SC-1 bites: the fleets' cold is chir
- **tev** — the lenition chain hides the root: tepa > teva > tev
- **mōr** — SC-5: mōr — a minimal pair with mor 'reef': the wave is the long rock
- **heban** — word-initial *h survives, hōl-class: the sky keeps its breath
- **nubes** — the *nub- root read upward: a cloud is a deep seen from below
- **zun** — the *dzu- 'adrift' family: night is the daily dark time
- **drem** — what you see in =zu time without leaving your bunk
- **stur** — the s-grade of *tur- 'tower': every ship carries a small tower
- **shechel** — unrecognizable by regular change alone: shechel
- **schir** — four cuts of a blade wrote jel; this is the blade itself
- **sheg** — s and g both shift: sheg
- **kov** — lenition: kov — a minimal pair with kav, the limit Κ
- **kat** — every hull carries one; the cat is exempt from muster
- **vejel** — two changes deep: vejel
- **kabal** — a ground-sider loan, and the shape shows it
- **rav** — lenition hides it: rav
- **bon** — the noun *bon- 'bone' and the verb *bon-a 'be sound' merge at the surface: soundness is bone-deep
- **telur** — the *tel- 'reach' root with the duration grade: far is a long reach
- **rej** — palatalized and clipped: rej
- **kaver** — the *kap- 'hold' root with the active grade — the limit Κ is a taking that never completes
- **stan** — the attested *stan 'station, stand' of Werstan, as a verb
- **nek** — one segment from *nex- 'the gap': to die is to almost-gap; fleet speech still prefers 'she has gone adrift'
- **log** — the Wolori's verb: Logos conjugates as log-a=mi
- **luv** — regular lenition: lupa > luva > luv
- **hov** — the mood -im is hope conjugated; this is hope as a verb
- **chent** — palatalized: chent
- **metur** — the -ur duration grade of *met-: a measure is a held count
- **steren** — what the navigator draws before the drive speaks
- **kilver** — what remains true when the voyage is over
