NUBHELTHE DEEP-SPEECH

The rival fleet's tongue — same proto, different wear, and a different physics grammaticalized: the divers built their verbs around the offset, not the gap

Nubhel [ˈnub.hel] (*nub-kel, "deep-speech") is Sūchel's sister and rival: the language of the Nūbi, the vertical fleet, who hold that the Speed Law's real teaching is depth, not crossing. Where the crossers live in the indeterminate regime — gaps, T•, unanchored time — the divers live in the determinate one: dilation, arithmetic, coming home older by a number. Their grammar shows it. Nubhel kept only three moods and cannot say the seam-truth; it replaced the dark anchor with a debt anchor; it grades depth five ways where Sūchel has two; and its speakers sign their names with their accrued cycles.

01 THE TWO FLEETS 二つの艦隊

Horizontal and vertical — one rivalry, two grammars

The split is older than either language remembers: one lineage bet on the seam, the other on the tower. Each thinks the other misread the Speed Law.

THE SĪLI · CROSSERS · SŪCHEL

The horizontal fleet. Seam-oriented; their prestige is the crossing and the T• mood, their wound is the gap. Their time is the indeterminate regime: lives punctuated by ne-holes no one can narrate. They call the divers nuvi — and, unkindly, "the aged," because divers come home old. Their sneer: "divers go fast to stay in one universe."

THE NŪBI · DIVERS · NUBHEL

The vertical fleet. Depth-oriented; their prestige is class — how deep a hull can hold — and their wound is the offset: they age past everyone they love, by a measurable count. They call the crossers Sūl — and, unkindly, "the gapped" or "hollow-lived," people whose autobiographies have holes. Their sneer: "crossing is a shortcut; depth is the truth." The Speed Law, read plainly, is on their side.

THE DESIGN THESIS

Same proto-language, same physics — but each fleet grammaticalized the regime it lives in. Sūchel built its verb around the seam: five truth-moods, an unanchorable tense, a particle for the un-narratable. Nubhel built its verb around the dive: fewer moods, a debt anchor, five degrees of depth, and the offset worn like a signature. Two daughters of one grammar, shaped by two kinds of time.

02 DIACHRONY 音変化

Seven changes — with one rule ordered before another

The deep set. Note D-3 is ordered before D-1: original *h dies first, then new h is born from *k — so every medial h in Nubhel is secondary, a diagnostic any philologist in your story can use. And where the capital shortened its vowels, the divers lengthened theirs: Nubhel is the tongue that holds its breath.

THE MINIMAL PAIR

*suw-kel gives Sūchel in one mouth and Sūhel in the other — the whole cognate gap in one phoneme. A crosser hears the divers name her language and hears it almost right, which is worse than wrong. Likewise the drive: *sow-orn → Sōrn in both — the family's most stable word. Everyone agrees on the engine.

TWO MERGERS, TWO JOKES

D-2 merges *wel "nothing" with *bel "hearth": in Nubhel, bel means both — the divers' own proverb, "the hearth is nothing to us," is an etymological pun. And D-4 merges *mar "pearl" into *mor "rock": mor covers both, and surface folk say the divers cannot tell a pearl from a stone — which the divers consider a compliment to their priorities.

03 THE GRAMMAR OF DEPTH 深度の文法

What the divers did to the four systems

Same inherited machine, rebuilt for the determinate regime. Verb template unchanged — STEM–(ASP)–MOOD=ANCHOR — but every slot drifted.

SYSTEM 01 · THREE MOODS — AND A HOLE

Nubhel kept three veridical moods: plain -ó (witnessed, settled), approach -im (nearing a limit — the divers' home mood), and hearsay -ur — old T⁺ "held-from-above" drifted to "held on another's word." But *-eshe left no reflex. Nubhel cannot conjugate the seam-truth.

on nub yed-ó=mi.3SG DOWN go-PLAIN=PROP"She went down." Witnessed, own-clock. The everyday music of the fleet.

The code-switch rule: a diver narrating a crossing must borrow Sūchel for the T• clause — mid-sentence, the rival's language appears exactly where the seam does. Enforced humility; on the page, a visible register shift in the balloon.

SYSTEM 02 · THE DEBT ANCHOR & THE SIGNATURE

Anchors =ka (beacon) and =mi (proper) are shared inheritance. But =zu has no Nubhel cognate — divers rarely leave coverage. In its place: =nu (from *nub), the deep-time anchor: "stated in dive-time, conversion owed." Formal register then appends the debt as a numeral — the offset signature.

en ōl-t-ó=nu, +mek-dok.1SG surface-PFV-PLAIN=DEEP, +four-ten"I surfaced — in dive-time; forty owed." Divers sign letters this way: Mora, +212 — a life's dilation worn as a name-suffix.
SYSTEM 03 · FIVE DEGREES OF DEPTH

Where Sūchel has two directionals, Nubhel grades five — motion verbs are ungrammatical without one:

SYSTEM 04 · NEKS — THE GAP AS COURTESY

The gap particle survives, heavier: neks (*nex-, cluster kept). But its pragmatics drifted. In Sūchel, ne marks what physics forbids narrating; in Nubhel, neks marks any interval your listener did not share — a returning diver summarizing years of away-time says it once and moves on. You do not narrate time your listener did not live.

en yed-t-ó=mi. neks. en len ve-ó=ka.1SG go-PFV-PLAIN=PROP · GAP · 1SG level be-PLAIN=BEAC"I left. — . I am here, level, in shared time." The homecoming formula: the away-years folded into one held mark, out of kindness.
THE RECONSTRUCTION PROOF — WHY -Ó MATTERS

Nubhel's plain mood is -ó where Sūchel has -á. D-4 rounds only stressed *a — so the suffix can only have become -ó if it already carried the stress when the daughters split. The sound change is fossil evidence that "the mood carries the beat" was late Old Pelagic prosody, not a Sūchel invention: both fleets inherited the truth-beat from the ancestor. One vowel, and the family's deepest rule is proven ancient.

06 TEXTS 例文

The deep-speech breathing

TEXT 01 // A DIVER'S HOMECOMING帰還

en len ve-ó=ka, +mek-dok kodur.

The whole determinate regime in two lines: the away-years folded into one neks out of courtesy, the return re-anchored to the beacon, and the dilation declared as a number. Where a crosser's homecoming ends in a question no arithmetic settles, a diver's ends in an invoice.

Roughly four words in five land across the gap — enmai, -mai, =mi and the shape of the verb are shared bones. What trips the ear is exactly the rivalry: nav/nā, nuv/nub, jed/yed, -a/-ó. Enough to trade, marry, and mishear a docking order at the worst possible moment.
