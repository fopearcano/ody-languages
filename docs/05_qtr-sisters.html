THE SISTERSOF OLD PELAGIC

One proto-tongue, a conquered universe of daughters — and the order that keeps the ancestor alive by refusing to let it pray

A civilization that can almost cross its universe entirely does not speak one language. It speaks a family: Sūchel binds the fleets, and every settled world keeps its own sister — same bones, different wear. Three archetypes are charted here at naming depth (a sound-change table and twenty place-names each): Rudgar, the conservative tongue of old ground on Mars; Sel, the liquid tongue of the ocean moon Maren; Beltsel, the eroded prestige tongue of the capital world Belgar. Beneath them all, Old Pelagic survives twice over — frozen in the Assembly's liturgy, and working in the hands of the Irrationals.

01 THE PALATAL SHIBBOLETH 合言葉

Place a stranger's homeworld in one word

The family's great isogloss is what each tongue did to old *k and *g before front vowels. Ask a stranger to name the seam, and their mouth files their birth certificate. Fleet ears do this without thinking; your panels can do it in one balloon.

02 RUDGAR — THE TONGUE OF OLD GROUND 火星語

Mars: the conservative sister

WHO · WHERE

Rudgar (*rud-gar "red ground" — the world and its tongue share a name) is spoken on Mars, the oldest continuously settled ground outside the birthworld. Its speakers stayed when everyone else left, and their language stayed with them: Rudgar is the family's conservative sister — it never lost *w or *h, the two sounds every other daughter killed. Martian ears are therefore the closest living ears to Old Pelagic; Assembly cantors and Wolori lectors alike are disproportionately Mars-born, because the proto-language sits easiest in a Rudgar mouth.

03 SEL — THE LIQUID SISTER 海月語

Maren: the tongue that drowned its glides

WHO · WHERE

Sel is spoken on Maren (*mar-en, "of the pearls"), an ocean moon whose people never left the water they colonized. Where Mars preserved, Maren dissolved: every *w and *h drowned into vowel length, stops soften between vowels, and old *k hisses to s before front vowels. The result is the family's most fluid sister — long, open, tide-paced. Fleet slang calls Sel speakers "the vowel-farmers"; Sel calls Sūchel "the tongue in a hurry."

04 BELTSEL — THE ERODED SISTER 首都語

Belgar: the capital that cannot hold its breath

WHO · WHERE

Beltsel (*bel-kel, "hearth-speech") is the prestige tongue of Belgar (*bel-gar, "Hearth-ground"), the administrative capital world. It is the family's fast sister — centuries of court, commerce, and bureaucracy sanded it down: unstressed vowels deleted, clusters crushed, and every long vowel shortened. That last change is the one the other sisters mock: Beltsel lost vowel length entirely — the capital cannot hold its breath. Fleet pilots, whose ū and ō are the tombstones of dead sounds, find capital speech faintly sacrilegious: a tongue with no room in it for a held silence.

05 THE WOLORI — LANGUAGE OF THE IRRATIONALS 無理数団の言葉

Yes: the old one — kept alive by refusing to let it pray

The order of science that follows Logos — the Ricercatori del Limite, called the Irrationals — does speak the ancestor. But Old Pelagic survives in two hands, and the difference between them is the whole politics of truth in this universe.

CUSTODIAN 01 · THE ASSEMBLY

Liturgical Pelgar — frozen. Performed, not lived; the grammatical claim that truth has not moved since before the seam. Doxa's instrument: the tongue of the consensus, whose creed is «tutto è connesso con tutto». It is sacred precisely because it is blind to the new world — it cannot conjugate a crossing, so in it no crossing can contradict doctrine.

CUSTODIAN 02 · THE WOLORI

Lorkel — working Old Pelagic. *lor-kel, "ratio-speech": the Irrationals' living technical register, extended by coinage the way Neo-Latin coined oxygenium. Same corpus, opposite use: the Assembly embalmed the ancestor; the Wolori keep it employed. Both claim custody; each calls the other's use profanation. The custody battle over a dead language is one of the quietest wars in the setting.

CUSTODIAN 00 · NOTATION

The zero-tongue. Above Lorkel sits the order's true liturgy: mathematics itself — no moods, no anchors, no native speakers. The only medium in which T• can be written without being sworn. Members take notation-names — √2, π, φ, √−1, z — pronounced differently on every world, written identically on all of them: the one perfect identity in a crossable universe.

WHY THE MOODLESS TONGUE IS THE SCIENTIFIC ONE

In Sūchel you cannot state a hypothesis. Every finite verb must swear a mood — T, T⁻, T⁺, or T• — so a conjecture is grammatical perjury and a heresy is two morphemes long. Old Pelagic predates the seam: no veridical moods, no temporal anchors, no ne. It is the only tongue in which a proposition can be entertained without being asserted — the hypothesis-space language, the laboratory glassware of speech.

Hence the mirrored joke at the heart of the family: the Assembly keeps Old Pelagic because it cannot describe the seam. The Wolori keep it for the same reason. One custodian uses the blindness to protect doctrine from evidence; the other uses it to protect inquiry from doctrine.

And the name completes the Pythagorean echo. Their endonym is Wolori — *wo-lor-i, "the un-ratioed" — Doxa's sneer worn as a badge, "Irrationals" being merely its translation. An order devoted to Logos, the Ratio, named for what escapes ratio: because their founding discovery — like √2 in a Pythagorean hand, like the verdict no beacon can vouch for — is that the truth is incommensurable with the consensus grammar. Bone's uniform bears «√2» for exactly this reason: the first number that broke the faith of the people who found it.

One more shibboleth for the page: a Wolori lector says gel, kel, Suwkel — the unshifted proto-forms. In a bar on any world, the order is audible in a single syllable, the way a Latin-dropping scholar is audible in ours.
